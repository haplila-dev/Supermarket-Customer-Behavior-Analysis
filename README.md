# Supermarket-Customer-Behavior-Analysis
Data analytics project exploring supermarket customer behavior, spending trends, and campaign performance.
Capstone Project Module 3 — Customer Analytics & Business Intelligence.

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes supermarket customer behavior using demographic information, spending activity, purchasing channels, and campaign response data. The objective is to understand customer characteristics, identify valuable customer segments, evaluate spending behavior, and provide business recommendations that can support marketing decisions.

---

## Business Background

The supermarket stores customer demographic and purchasing information, including:

- Customer income
- Product spending
- Purchase channels
- Campaign responses
- Complaint history

Understanding customer behavior helps businesses:

- Identify valuable customers
- Discover spending patterns
- Improve campaign targeting
- Support data-driven decisions

---

## Business Questions

1. Who are the supermarket customers?
2. Which customer segments contribute the highest value?
3. Which products generate the highest spending?
4. What purchasing behaviors do customers have?
5. Which customers respond better to campaigns?

---

## Key Findings

- Medium-value customers account for approximately 50% of customers
- Wine products generate the highest spending (~680K)
- Store purchases dominate customer transactions (~13K)
- High-value customers show stronger campaign response
- Customers rarely depend on discounts
